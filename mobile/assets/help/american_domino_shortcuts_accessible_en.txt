1. Game Table Shortcuts:
• P: Announce the list and number of players present at the table.
• T: Announce the name of the player whose turn it is.
• R: Announce the open domino ends and their sum to score multiples of five.
• C: Announce the number of tiles remaining in the draw (bank).
• S: Announce the total points each player has in the match.
• V: Show the list of tiles played on the table.
2. Play and Draw Shortcuts:
• Up and Down Arrows: Navigate between your domino tiles.
• Enter: Play the currently selected tile.
• Space or D: Draw from the bank when you do not have a matching tile.
3. Game Management Shortcuts (for the room host):
• Enter: Start the game when positioned on the table item.
• Shift + X: End the current game.
• B: Add a robot (bot) player to the room.
• Shift + B: Remove the last added bot.
