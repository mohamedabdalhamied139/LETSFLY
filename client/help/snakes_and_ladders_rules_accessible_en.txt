Comprehensive Snakes and Ladders Guide and Rules (The Hundred-Square Journey)

Part One: Introduction and Basic Gameplay

Game Concept and Main Objective
Snakes and Ladders is the most famous and entertaining classic racing game in the world, filled with surprises and unexpected turns.
The game consists of a board with squares numbered in ascending order from square number 1 to square number 100.
All players start outside the board at point zero.
The main objective is to roll the dice and advance through the board's path to be the first player to reach exactly square number 100.

Turn Sequence and Movement
The turn passes among the players sequentially in a clockwise direction.
On your turn, you press the roll dice option or the Enter key.
The dice generates a fair random number from 1 to 6.
Your token advances on the board by a number of steps equal to the number rolled, accompanied by the sound of movement steps and the announcement of the new square number you landed on.

Practical Example of Starting a Game Between Two Players:
Suppose the match started between two players, and both are at point zero.
The first player rolled the dice and got the number 5.
What happens?
The first player advances five steps and lands on square number 5, and the turn passes to the second player.

Practical Example of Gameplay Between Three Players:
Suppose the first player is on square 10, the second player is on square 15, and the third player is on square 20.
The second player rolled the dice and got the number 3.
What happens?
The second player advances 3 steps to land on square 18 (15 plus 3 equals 18), then the turn passes to the third player.


Part Two: Classic Ladders and Snakes

Ladders and the Rocket Ascent Path
The board contains eight ladders placed in specific and fixed squares.
If your dice movement ends exactly at the base of a ladder, you instantly climb the ladder to reach its top in one step, saving you massive distances!
Locations of the eight ladders and their values:
First Ladder: From square 4 climbs you to square 14.
Second Ladder: From square 9 climbs you to square 31.
Third Ladder: From square 20 climbs you to square 38.
Fourth Ladder: From square 28 climbs you to square 84.
Fifth Ladder: From square 40 climbs you to square 59.
Sixth Ladder: From square 51 climbs you to square 67.
Seventh Ladder: From square 63 climbs you to square 81.
Eighth Ladder: From square 71 climbs you to square 91.

Snakes and Backward Sliding Bites
The board contains eight cunning snakes lurking for advancing players.
If your dice movement ends exactly at the head of a snake, you instantly slide down with the sound of a snake bite, dropping to its tail in a lower square, sending you backward!
Locations of the eight snakes and their results:
First Snake: On square 17 bites you down to square 7.
Second Snake: On square 54 bites you down to square 34.
Third Snake: On square 62 bites you down to square 19.
Fourth Snake: On square 64 bites you down to square 60.
Fifth Snake: On square 87 bites you down to square 24.
Sixth Snake: On square 93 bites you down to square 73.
Seventh Snake: On square 95 bites you down to square 75.
Eighth Snake: On square 98 bites you down to square 79.

Practical Example of Climbing a Ladder:
Suppose a player is standing on square number 24, and rolled the dice and got the number 4.
What happens to the player?
The player advances 4 steps and reaches square 28 (24 plus 4 equals 28).
Since square 28 is the base of a giant ladder, the player instantly climbs to the top of the ladder at square 84 and becomes very close to the finish line!

Practical Example of a Snake Bite:
Suppose a player was on square number 83, and rolled the dice and got the number 4.
What happens to the player?
The player advances 4 steps and reaches square 87 (83 plus 4 equals 87).
Since square 87 contains a snake's head, the snake bites them instantly and they slide down to its tail on square 24!


Part Three: Special Dice Rules and Deciding the End

The Rule of Number 6 and the Free Extra Roll
If you roll the dice and get the number 6:
You advance 6 full steps forward.
You immediately receive a bonus, which is a free extra roll in the same turn to roll again and advance further!

The Penalty Rule of Three Consecutive Sixes
If you are lucky enough to roll the number 6 twice in a row and then roll the number 6 for a third consecutive time in the same turn:
The game considers this excessive greed, and completely cancels all your moves made during this turn.
The game immediately returns you to the square you were standing on before the turn started, and your turn ends automatically and passes to the next player!

The Rule of Reaching Square 100 Exactly and Exceeding the End
To win the match, the game requires reaching square number 100 with the exact remaining number without excess:
If the number rolled exceeds square 100 (for example, you were on square 97 and rolled a 5, totaling 102), your move is considered invalid and you do not move a single step. You remain fixed in your place on square 97, and the game announces that no move is possible and passes the turn to the next player.
There is no backward bouncing upon exceeding one hundred; the player simply remains standing in place until they get the exact roll.

Practical Example of Attempting to Win and Exceeding the End:
Suppose a player is standing on square number 98, and needs exactly two steps to win.
If the player rolls and gets the number 3 (98 plus 3 equals 101):
The player does not move at all, remains on square 98, and the turn passes to the next player.
However, if on their next turn they roll exactly the number 2 (98 plus 2 equals 100):
They reach square 100 and win the match instantly!


Part Four: Advanced Modes (Knockout and Mystery Tiles)

Knockout and Attack Mode (Knockout Mode)
When this mode is activated in the room settings:
If your dice movement ends exactly on the same square occupied by an opposing player, you forcibly knock them out, sending them 10 steps backward towards the start!

Mystery Tiles Mode (Mystery Tiles Mode)
Random gift and surprise boxes are scattered across the board, which continuously regenerate whenever opened by a player.
When you land on a mystery box, it opens one of the following surprises for you:
1. Shield: Grants you a shield that automatically protects you from the next bite of any snake and destroys the snake instead of you sliding down.
2. Freeze Trap: Freezes you in a block of ice for a full turn, causing you to skip your next turn without playing until the ice melts on the turn after.
3. Boost: Instantly propels you forward by an additional 3 to 7 steps.
4. Magic Swap: Magically and instantly swaps your position with the leading player in the game, taking their advanced spot!
5. Golden Dice (Bonus Roll): Grants you an immediate free extra dice roll.
6. Wind Storm: A storm blows you backward by 2 to 4 steps.
7. Coin Box (Coins): Grants you a balance of coins added to your account.

Practical Example of Knockout Mode:
Suppose the second player is standing on square 50, and the first player was standing on square 46 and rolled a 4.
The first player reaches square 50 and knocks out the second player, pushing them 10 steps backward so the second player lands on square 40!

Practical Example of the Swap Box:
Suppose the third player was behind on square 12, and the leader was on square 85.
The third player landed on a mystery box and got the magic position swap:
The third player instantly moves to square 85, and the leader drops to square 12!
