Ninety-Nine Game Shortcuts:
1. Play Shortcuts:
• Left and Right Arrows: Navigate between hand cards.
• Enter: Play the selected card and add it to the total.
• Space: Draw a card from the pile (in draw mode).
• S: Announce the current total and remaining points for players.
• P: Announce the list of players at the table.
• T: Announce the name of the player whose turn it is currently.
• C: Announce the last card played on the table.

2. Game Management Shortcuts (For Room Owner):
• Shift + X: Stop the game and return to waiting.
• B: Add a bot player.
• Shift + B: Remove the last added bot.
