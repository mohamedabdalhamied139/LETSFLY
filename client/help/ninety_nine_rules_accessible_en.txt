Explanation and Rules of the Ninety-Nine Game:
Goal of the Game:
Maintain the cumulative total under or equal to 99. The player forced to exceed the total of 99 loses a point (a life). The last player holding a life wins the game!

Rules of Play:
1. Each player starts with three cards and three points (lives).
2. On your turn, you play one card from your hand, which is added to the shared cumulative total, and then you draw a new card.
3. If your card causes the total to exceed 99, you lose a point and the round ends.
4. When a player loses all three of their lives, they are eliminated from the match.

Values of Special Cards:
• Ace: Adds 1 or 11 (at the player's choice).
• Cards 2 to 8: Add their face value directly.
• 9: Pass card (passes the turn without changing the total).
• 10: Subtracts 10 from the cumulative total.
• Jack: Adds 10.
• Queen: Adds 10.
• King: Directly sets the cumulative total to 99!
• 4: Reverses the direction of play.
