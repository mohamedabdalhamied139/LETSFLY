1. Dice Rolling and Playing Shortcuts:
• Enter: Roll the dice and advance on the board.
2. Inquiry and Location Shortcuts:
• P: Announce the list and number of players present at the table.
• T: Announce the name of the player whose turn it currently is.
• R: Board radar (know the closest ladder or snake to you).
• V: Announce the locations and squares of all players on the board.
• S: Announce the players' locations and the goal (square 100).
• D: Re-announce the last dice roll.
3. Game Management Shortcuts (for the room host):
• Enter: Start the game when standing on the table item.
• Shift + X: End the current game.
• B: Add a robot (bot) player to the room.
• Shift + B: Remove the last added bot.
