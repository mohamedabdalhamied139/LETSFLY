1. Play Table Shortcuts:
• P: Announce the list and number of players present at the table.
• T: Announce the name of the player whose turn it currently is.
• R: Announce the ends of the open domino pieces on the table.
• C: Announce the number of remaining pieces in the draw (bank).
• S: Announce the total points each player has in the match.
• V: Display the list of pieces played on the table.
2. Playing and Drawing Shortcuts:
• Up and Down Arrows: Navigate between your domino pieces.
• Enter: Play the currently selected piece.
• Space or D: Draw from the bank when you do not have a matching piece.
3. Game Management Shortcuts (for the room host):
• Enter: Start the game when standing on the table item.
• Shift + X: End the current game.
• B: Add a robot (bot) player to the room.
• Shift + B: Remove the last added bot.
